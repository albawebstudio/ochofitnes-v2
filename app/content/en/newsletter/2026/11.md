## About me:

A lot has happened family! First let me tell you that the kids arrived in Mexico City in June and were able to stay until August 11, 2026. I had a wonderful time with them. I hate when they have to go back. I miss them so much. A lot has been going on since then. I stopped writing the newsletters first because I was depressed when the kids left. Then in October some of my computer’s keys broke I’m back now thank God. So, some updates: I’m working with my mom’s immigration attorney to see if I qualify to apply to return home sooner. My oldest son stayed in Oaxaca with me, and he is pursuing a soccer career. He will try out for one of Oaxaca’s professional soccer team in December.

[Plantilla · Dragones TDP | Dragones Oaxaca](https://www.dragonesfc.mx/equipos/liga-tdp/plantilla)

In September Angelo and me were invited to celebrate my Tio Rene’s birthday in Puerto Escondido. We had an amazing time with the De la Cruz family. It also made me miss the kids so much. What a blessing that Angelo was there with me. That was amazing.

In October, I turned 41! My grandma and family came to Mexico to celebrate her birthday. Angelo, my brother, and me 
were invited to the big party. It was great to hug my grandma and aunts. It doesn’t get easier when people return home and I have to stay here. Eventually people say you just except it. For the first time I even got a few dances in with my aunt and Lili. Such a sweet lady.

In October, I also ran into an old friend Armando from California who is now living in Oaxaca. What a great man with 
amazing stories. He has a coffee shop in Oaxaca: Cocos café.

Angelo and I were able to celebrate Día de Muertos in Oaxaca. It was beautiful. I even ran into a lovely young lady from Des Moines, Iowa. She was traveling solo what a savage. In November, I was able to celebrate Angelo’s birthday too!

I still make time to visit my God parents in San Pablo Huixtepec. In October and November, I dealt with some injuries that stopped me from doing my run club. Healing nicely.

I attend Angelo’s training Monday-Friday. I’m fortunate to be able to do that.

Thank you, my Ocho Fitness family, for everything. I appreciate and love you very much.
